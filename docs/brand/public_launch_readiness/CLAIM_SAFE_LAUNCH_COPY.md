## 8. What NOT to say (short list)

- Do NOT say "BIZRA is live" → say "BIZRA is public" or "The Seed is public."
- Do NOT cite SNR 0.974, $0.10→$0.008, 100% pass, 8072 tests, 73/100 nodes in ads.
- Do NOT imply production readiness, security certification, AGI, financial return, or "first/best."
- Do NOT use "guaranteed," "proven better than," "no risk," "will save you money."
- Do NOT use the internal PAT/SAT terminology in consumer copy.
- Do NOT break English/Arabic parity (if EN says ambition, AR must say ambition — not a fact).
