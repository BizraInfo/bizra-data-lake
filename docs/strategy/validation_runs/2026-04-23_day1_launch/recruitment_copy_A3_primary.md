# Recruitment Copy — A3 Primary Segment

**Segment:** A3 — AI-native creator / consultant (primary, target 10 interviews)
**Hard filter (GUARDRAIL 2):** client-facing + AI in delivery + ≥1 credibility/proof/revision dispute in last 90 days
**Compensation offered:** $50 gift card, sent after interview
**Tone rule:** plain, not hype. No BIZRA marketing. Research-positioned.

---

**Usage:** one-to-one only. Do not copy-paste blast. Personalize the first line to why you're reaching out to *them* specifically.

## 4. Screening form text (4 items — hard filter)

Deploy as Google Form, Typeform, or similar. Do not substitute for a call — use as pre-call filter.

**Intake: AI & client-facing work — 4 questions**

> Thanks for your interest. Four quick questions before we book a time.

**Q1. Client-facing work.**
> Do you currently deliver work to paying clients or external stakeholders who receive specific deliverables from you? (Yes / No)

**Q2. AI in delivery.**
> In the last 30 days, has an AI tool (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor, Copilot, etc.) contributed directly to what you delivered — not just to your research or note-taking? If yes, which tool(s)?
> *[short answer]*

**Q3. Recurring.**
> Is AI-assisted work a recurring part of your workflow, weekly or more often? (Yes / No)

**Q4. Recent incident — HARD FILTER.**
> In the last 90 days, has a client, collaborator, or reviewer challenged you on what the AI did, whether it was correct, or whether it should have been used?
> If YES, please briefly describe: when, who asked, and what they were asking about. (A sentence or two is fine — no sensitive details needed.)
> *[short answer]*

**End of form.** Candidate must answer Q1=Yes, Q2=Yes, Q3=Yes, AND provide a specific dated incident in Q4 to qualify.

## 5. Disqualifier notes (for operator)

If the candidate:

| Condition | Action |
|---|---|
| Q1 = No (no external deliverables, internal work only) | Thank and release. Not a fit for A3. |
| Q2 = No (AI used only for research, not in delivery) | Thank and release. Weak fit. |
| Q3 = No (one-off AI experimenter, not recurring) | Thank and release. Not a rich enough signal. |
| Q4 = No (no 90-day incident) | **Thank and release — hard disqualification.** Do not rebook under a "softer" criterion. Weak-S4 contamination is the biggest risk. |
| Q4 = "abstractly yes" / "clients worry about it" / "not a specific incident but..." | **Disqualify.** Require a specific dated example. |
| Q4 = YES with a real incident | → schedule interview. High priority. |
| Candidate is employed by Anthropic, OpenAI, Google DeepMind, Meta AI, xAI, or similar AI-vendor company | Disqualify — strategic-interest contamination. |
| Candidate is a known BIZRA contributor, prior consultant to the founder, or friends-of-the-founder | Disqualify — signal contamination. |
| Candidate is 2nd-degree social connection from founder | Allow, but flag in outreach tracker. Aim for ≥ 6 interviews from non-1st-degree intros total. |

### What to do with disqualified candidates

- Thank them genuinely.
- Do NOT "convert" them to A1 or A8 just to book them — segment discipline matters more than volume.
- Offer to follow up in a later sprint if they gain a qualifying incident later.
- Do not send them to the screening form a second time.

## 6. Operator notes

- **$50 is paid AFTER the interview, not before.** Paying before biases response.
- **Do not hint at BIZRA.** If the candidate asks "what tool?", say: "I'll describe the specific project at the end of the call — I want your current experience to stay uncontaminated by my framing."
- **Capture consent explicitly** at the start of the call. Without it, the interview cannot go into the evidence log.
- **Each interview becomes a `Template 1` artifact in the validation_runs folder** named `INT-<date>-<segment>-<sequence>.md` per the convention.
- **Wording defaults to "signed record"** per GUARDRAIL 3 at Phase 4 artifact reaction.
- **Every interview must produce ≥1 disconfirming signal.** If it doesn't, the interviewer was leading.
