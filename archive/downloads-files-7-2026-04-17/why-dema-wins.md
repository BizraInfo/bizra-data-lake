**One sentence:** Dema is the sovereign face of a constitutional trust compiler that turns human intent into lawful, receipted, replayable action.

## The problem no one else is solving

Every AI tool today has the same architecture: you type something, a model responds, and you trust the output because you trust the provider. There is no proof. There is no receipt. There is no way to verify that the system did what it said, followed the rules it claimed, or produced the quality it promised.

When these tools fail, they fail silently. When they succeed, you can't prove it to anyone else.

## What Dema does differently

Dema is a CLI (and eventually a visual interface) that sits on top of a constitutional runtime. Every action passes through five invariant gates before it executes. Every execution produces a cryptographic receipt. Every receipt enters a chain that any node can replay independently.

**Three properties no competitor has:**

**1. Law before intelligence.** Before Dema does anything, the system evaluates whether the action is admissible — does it meet the quality floor (Ihsan ≥ 0.95)? Does it carry evidence (Zann Zero)? Is it non-extractive (Riba Zero)? Does the claim bind to proof? Does the interface match the kernel state? If any gate fails, the action is rejected with a remediation path. The rejection never enters the chain. The chain only contains truth.

**2. One face, hidden council.** The operator sees Dema. Dema alone. Behind Dema, a 7-agent personal team (PAT-7) handles planning, research, coding, evaluation, ethics, publishing, and integration. A 5-agent system team (SAT-5) handles consensus, resources, proof, impact, and governance. The operator never manages agents. The operator states intent; Dema compiles it into lawful action.

**3. Every action receipted.** Not logged — receipted. A BLAKE3-hashed, chain-linked, independently-replayable proof artifact that binds the claim to its evidence, the evidence to its execution, and the execution to its result. Any auditor, regulator, or future node can verify the entire history without trusting anyone.

## Who this is for

Dema is for anyone who needs to prove their AI did what it said it did: regulated industries (finance, healthcare, legal), sovereign infrastructure operators, independent professionals who own their own data, and anyone who believes trust should be earned by design, not promised by policy.
