# Cost Management (FinOps) Plan (Template)

## KPIs (examples)
- Cost per 1k tokens per provider/model
- Cost per active user per week
- Cost per successful synthesis

## Budgets and enforcement
- Per-user daily cap (Alpha)
- Project/org cap (monthly)
- Global hard cap with graceful degradation (local models or reduced quality mode)

## Alerts
- 50/75/90% monthly budget alerts
- Burn-rate alerts (spend/hour)
- Anomaly detection (endpoint/provider spikes)
