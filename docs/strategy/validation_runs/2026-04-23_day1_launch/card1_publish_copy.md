## 5. Five likely replies — and how to classify them

### Reply Type 1 — Vague enthusiasm
> *"Cool, sounds useful. What's the pricing?"*

**Classification: WEAK.** No named incident, no workflow-change statement. Do NOT count as signal.

**Operator response:**
> Thanks for the interest. Before pricing — my research is specifically about consultants who've been challenged on AI work recently. Has a client or collaborator pushed back on you about AI in the last 90 days? If yes, happy to book a research call. If not, I'll reach out when the tool is closer to launch.

**Rule:** this response filters enthusiasts from S4-qualified candidates. Do not soften.

---

### Reply Type 2 — Named incident + workflow-change (THE GOLD REPLY)
> *"Oh my god yes, last month my client asked me to show how the AI produced a report and I spent 4 hours reconstructing screenshots. If I had something like this I'd drop the screenshot habit in a heartbeat."*

**Classification: STRONG.** Named dated incident ("last month"), named action ("asked me to show how"), workflow-change statement ("drop the screenshot habit"). This is the highest-value reply type.

**Operator response:**
> That's exactly the kind of story I'm researching. Send you the 4-question intake + we book a time. $50 for your time afterward.

**Book this interview first.** Mark as high priority in outreach tracker.

---

### Reply Type 3 — Disconfirming
> *"I don't think my clients would care about this — they just want results."*

**Classification: DISCONFIRMING (D-grade).** Log in outreach tracker AND note it as message-test disconfirm count for CARD_1.

**Operator response:**
> That's useful to hear. Quick follow-up if you're open: what kinds of client engagements, if any, would you expect this to matter in? And if none — what would a client have to ask for you to feel you needed a record?

This captures the *shape* of the disconfirm. Do not argue. Do not defend. Thank and log.

**Rule:** ≥ 5 disconfirms on CARD_1 = drop the frame and re-examine H1 for this channel.

---

### Reply Type 4 — Feature-expansion request (no pain anchor)
> *"Can it integrate with my Notion / Zapier / [tool]?"*

**Classification: WEAK / no-fit.** Feature expansion without pain corroboration.

**Operator response:**
> Before features — my research is about whether the underlying problem is real. Has a client pushed back on something AI contributed to your work in the last 90 days? If yes, I'd love to hear that specific story first.

Do NOT commit to feature integrations. Do NOT pitch BIZRA. Redirect to pain-discovery.

---

### Reply Type 5 — Adjacent-segment pull (enterprise / regulated)
> *"I work in legal / finance / healthcare and we need audit trails. Let's talk."*

**Classification: STRONG but adjacent-segment (A5 or A7, not A3 first-validation).**

**Operator response:**
> Appreciate that. Your context is adjacent to my current research but valuable. I can book a 30-minute call separately — tagged differently from my primary cohort, and without the gift card structure. My primary sprint right now is A3 consultants. Would you prefer that 30-minute call now, or a more detailed enterprise conversation in a few weeks once I have more to show?

**Operator rule:** book the call if time allows, but **tag it `A5_adjacent` or `A7_adjacent` in the outreach tracker and do NOT include in the A3 interview total** (max 10 A3 external interviews of record). A5/A7 inbound is valuable inbound signal but not first-sprint evidence.

## 7. Explicit operator note — the dividing line

**Engagement without workflow-change implication is not strong signal.**

- A LinkedIn post that gets 200 likes and 50 "sounds great!" replies and 0 named incidents = **WEAK**.
- A LinkedIn post that gets 30 likes and 3 named-incident replies with workflow-change statements = **STRONG**.

Do not confuse volume with signal. The card is doing its job only if it produces people who can name a real 90-day incident and describe what they'd change in their workflow to get a signed record.

If after 72 hours of CARD_1 publication, there are zero named-incident replies — the frame is weak and we should not wait for more volume.
