## 1. Raw source — preserved exactly (الأصل)

**Do not correct. Do not normalize. Do not add tashkeel. Do not rephrase.**

```
كلما ازدت علما ازدت يقين بجهلى
وازدت يقين ان راى صواب يحتمل الخطا وان راى غيرى خطا يحتمل الصواب
وايضا قانون الافتراض هوا اننا لا نفترض ولا نقبل الظن المجرد
بل اذا كان الافتراض والظن امر واجب لا مفر منه فانن نقوم بذلك باحسان
```

This is Mumu's voice as given. Any edit — even to "improve" spelling or grammar — changes the provenance. The polished form belongs in the publication layer (see §3). The raw form lives here.

## 2. Faithful English rendering (ترجمة أمينة للأصل)

```
The more I learn, the more certain I become of my ignorance.
And the more certain I become that my view is right but may be wrong —
and another's view is wrong but may be right.
And also: the Law of Assumption — we do not assume,
and we do not accept bare speculation.
But when assumption and speculation become an unavoidable matter,
we do so with Ihsan.
```

This rendering preserves Mumu's structure and rhythm — including the *connector-driven* build ("وازدت", "وايضا", "بل") which the polished form flattens into prose.

## 4. Three invariants inside the kernel

The raw utterance contains three stacked operational invariants. Every downstream enforcement trace back to exactly one of them:

### 4.1 Knowledge → humility (not arrogance)

> كلما ازدت علما ازدت يقين بجهلى

The learning loop inverts confidence. More knowledge = more certainty about one's own ignorance, not more certainty about one's own rightness.

**Enforced by:**
- Brand canon §6.2 "Humility — التواضع المعرفي"
- Audit engine's refusal to credit exact metric claims without receipts
- `GOLDEN_GEMS_REGISTER.md` gem #6 "Law of Assumption"

### 4.2 Symmetric epistemic charity

> ان راى صواب يحتمل الخطا وان راى غيرى خطا يحتمل الصواب

My view is right-but-may-err. Another's view is wrong-but-may-hold-truth. Anti-dogmatic **by construction**. The structure is symmetric — both poles hold both possibilities.

**Enforced by:**
- Brand canon §10 Dema voice ("truthful mirror, disciplined guide" — not "authority")
- `IHSAN_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT.md` §3 Human dignity — refusing to treat users as raw material for opinion
- Reviewer-discipline feedback memories (land-the-plane, supersede-over-stack, third-party-eval-not-authorization) — each is a concrete application of this symmetry

### 4.3 The Law of Assumption with an Ihsan escape clause

> قانون الافتراض هوا اننا لا نفترض ولا نقبل الظن المجرد
> بل اذا كان الافتراض والظن امر واجب لا مفر منه فانن نقوم بذلك باحسان

Default: refuse assumption, refuse bare speculation. When forced into assumption by necessity: do it **with Ihsan** — with conscience, precision, dignity, and declared uncertainty.

**Enforced by:**
- `tools/audit/omni_audit/claim_scanner.py` — downgrades exact-metric claims without receipts to `NEEDS_REWRITE`
- `docs/brand/public_launch_readiness/PUBLIC_CLAIMS_REGISTER.md` A/B/C/D/E classification
- `docs/audits/omnidirectional_hyperdimensional_audit_v0_1/p0_bulletproofing/p0_2_site_claims/` P0.2 site cleanup
- Cognitive Foundry canon pack discipline: every pack is **candidate for** canon, never canon itself; the Canon Store Ingestion Gate is the required boundary
- `CLAIM_DISCIPLINE.md` (inside launch media kit): "If not measured, phrase as direction. If measured, cite receipt. If uncertain, mark uncertain."
