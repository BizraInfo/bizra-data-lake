## 2. Problem Statement

### 2.1 The Governance Vacuum
AI agents today operate without constitutional governance. OpenClaw had 42,665 exposed instances with 93.4% showing auth bypass. Agent Zero's README states: "There are no hard-coded rails that agents have to follow." The industry built `exec()` without building `chmod`.

### 2.2 The Trust Deficit
No AI platform can prove what it did. ChatGPT doesn't sign its outputs. Devin doesn't chain its receipts. When an AI agent says "I organized your files," there's no cryptographic proof it happened.

### 2.3 The Fairness Gap
AI token economics are speculation-driven. Tokens are pre-mined for insiders, lack real utility, and have no sinks. The gaming industry solved token economics 20 years ago — AI hasn't adopted those lessons.

- SAT cannot initiate — only responds through constitutional gates
- Permission funnel: Human → DEMA → PAT-7 → Pool → SAT-5

### 3.4 SEED Token Economy

- **Sources**: Missions, verified code, compute provision, discovery, agent training
- **Sinks**: Agent compute costs, Zakat (2.5%), Harberger (5%), skill marketplace, Telescript raids
