# Go-To-Market Plan for Node0 Activation

## Positioning

BIZRA is the Seed of Sovereign Intelligence: a human-first AI ecosystem built on meaning, proof, and Ihsan. The GTM wedge is not "another chatbot". It is proof-bearing, human-owned, local-first agency.

## Beachhead

Start with trusted builders, founders, researchers, and sovereignty-sensitive operators who understand why local control, receipts, and claim discipline matter.

Primary early segments:

- Technical founders and builders in the Gulf.
- AI governance and trust leaders.
- Sovereignty/privacy-sensitive professionals.
- Islamic finance / ethical technology audiences.
- Family/community productivity users after usability improves.

## Core Narrative

Before code, BIZRA was the Seed. Now the Seed has become a working sovereign-node architecture. The next step is not hype. The next step is proof: real devices, real receipts, real operator discipline.

## Launch Lanes

### Lane 1: Private Pilot

Goal: prove multiple devices can join a controlled pilot and verify signed receipts.

Channels:

- Direct founder outreach.
- Trusted WhatsApp/Telegram groups.
- Personal network.
- Closed demo calls.

### Lane 2: Organic Public Brand

Goal: introduce the BIZRA identity safely without overclaiming production network readiness.

Channels:

- X / LinkedIn / Instagram.
- Founder letter.
- First-look video.
- Website hero cleanup.

Use copy from `docs/brand/public_launch_readiness/CLAIM_SAFE_LAUNCH_COPY.md`.

### Lane 3: Investor / Partner Outreach

Goal: secure strategic partners, pilot users, or advisors around the verified wedge.

Materials:

- `INVESTOR_OPERATOR_HANDOVER.md`.
- Pilot evidence pack.
- Strategy memo.
- Business model options.

### Lane 4: Paid Readiness

Goal: prepare paid ads only after risk gates are green.

Blocked until:

- Claim cleanup on bizra.ai.
- Privacy policy published or claims softened.
- Creative visual QA.
- Arabic review.
- Platform setup and 2FA.
- UTM conventions.
- Kill-switch documented.

## Funnel

| Stage | Objective | CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Explain Seed, sovereignty, proof, Ihsan | Read manifesto |
| Interest | Show Node0 truth-labeled status | Join private pilot waitlist |
| Evaluation | Demonstrate receipt loop | Book pilot call |
| Activation | Onboard trusted device | Complete readiness check |
| Evidence | Verify signed receipt exchange | Add to pilot scorecard |

## 30-Day GTM Actions

- Publish claim-safe website hero or staging page.
- Prepare private pilot invite copy.
- Select 10 candidate pilots, onboard 2-5.
- Produce first pilot evidence pack.
- Update investor handover with measured results.

## Success Metrics

- Number of qualified pilot conversations.
- Number of devices passing readiness.
- Number of signed receipt handshakes completed.
- Time to onboard a device.
- Number of support issues by category.
- Organic engagement quality, not vanity impressions.

## No-Go Conditions

Do not scale GTM if:

- Pilot devices cannot verify receipts.
- Onboarding requires undocumented operator improvisation.
- Public site still contains unsupported production claims.
- Privacy and incident handling are unclear.
