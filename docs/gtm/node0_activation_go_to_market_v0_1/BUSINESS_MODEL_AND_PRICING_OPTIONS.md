# Business Model and Pricing Options

## Strategic Principle

Do not monetize network-scale promises before network-scale evidence exists. Monetize the verified wedge first: sovereign-node setup, proof-bearing workflows, and operator-grade deployments.

## Near-Term Business Models

### 1. Private Pilot / Founder Circle

Offer a limited, high-touch private pilot for trusted early users.

Value:

- Early access to sovereign-node workflow.
- Direct operator support.
- Influence on onboarding and product direction.
- Evidence-first participation.

Pricing stance:

- Free or symbolic for close trusted pilots.
- Paid only when support obligations, data boundaries, and deliverables are clear.

### 2. Sovereign Node Setup Service

A services-led model for individuals or organizations that want a private node configured on their hardware.

Value:

- Install and readiness setup.
- Identity and receipt verification.
- Local-first workflow guidance.
- Operator training.

Revenue:

- One-time setup fee.
- Optional support retainer.

### 3. Enterprise / Institutional Pilot

For regulated or sovereignty-sensitive teams.

Value:

- Local deployment assessment.
- Proof-bearing agent workflow prototype.
- Governance and claim-discipline review.
- Custom evidence report.

Revenue:

- Paid pilot fee.
- Deployment retainer.
- Future license or managed support.

### 4. Future URP Resource Pool

Longer-term model once multiple nodes are measured.

Value:

- Resource discovery.
- Verified contribution.
- Receipt-backed work exchange.
- Fairness and governance primitives.

Status:

- Directional until production bootnode, peer attestation, and multi-node receipt exchange are proven.

## Business Model Recommendation

Start with services and private pilots. This aligns revenue with truth: the customer pays for setup, proof workflows, and governance expertise, not for an unproven public network.

## Pricing Architecture Draft

| Package | Buyer | Offer | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder Pilot | Trusted individuals | Private node onboarding and feedback loop | Now, limited |
| Node Setup | Builders and power users | Guided install, local proof flow, support | After first pilot evidence |
| Sovereign Workflow Pilot | Teams | One proof-bearing workflow on local infrastructure | After docs and support are mature |
| Enterprise Deployment | Institutions | Custom node deployment, governance, training | After repeated pilot success |
| URP Marketplace | Network participants | Resource contribution and discovery | Future, after measured multi-node layer |

## Claims to Avoid in Sales

- Guaranteed returns.
- Fully decentralized today.
- Trustless network today.
- Production federation today.
- Works on every device today.
- Security certification unless formally issued.

## Commercial KPI

The first commercial KPI is not revenue. It is paid or trusted users who complete the sovereign-node proof loop and can explain the value in their own words.
