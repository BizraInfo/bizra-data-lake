# SECTION 2: IMPLEMENTATION IN THE CONSTELLATION

## 2.1 SAPE-Driven Reasoning
Agents are instructed to use the SAPE layers during `GoT` (Graph-of-Thoughts) reasoning:
1. **Gather Symbolic Evidence**: Use `MEASURED` and `IMPLEMENTED` tags.
2. **Form Abstractions**: Use `DERIVED` and `DESIGNED` tags.
3. **Execute Probes**: Challenge the abstractions using the `Adaptive Orchestrator`.
4. **Achieve Elevation**: The `Polymath Integrator` synthesizes the final wisdom.

## 2.2 SNR as a SAPE Metric
- **Symbolic SNR**: Accuracy of data and code.
- **Abstraction SNR**: Clarity of patterns and models.
- **Probe SNR**: Depth of critical questioning.
- **Elevation SNR**: Actionability and wisdom of the final output.
