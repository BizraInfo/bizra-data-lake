Conduct comprehensive research on: $ARGUMENTS

## Research Protocol
1. **Define Scope**: What exactly needs to be researched?
2. **Local Search**: Check codebase, docs, previous work
3. **External Search**: Web search for latest information
4. **Triangulate**: Cross-reference multiple sources
5. **Synthesize**: Build coherent understanding
6. **Cite**: Provide full provenance for all claims

## Output
- Executive summary
- Detailed findings (organized by theme)
- Key insights and recommendations
- Source bibliography
- Confidence assessment
